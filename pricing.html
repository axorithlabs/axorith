FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Axorith really free right now?

Yes! Until February 2026, there are no limits — use unlimited presets, all features, everything. We're in alpha and want you to test everything.

What happens when pricing launches?

Early users who signed up before February 2026 will receive 3 months of Pro for free as a thank you for being early adopters.

What's included in the free version?

You get access to ALL modules (Spotify, Site Blocker, App Launcher, etc.), the full session engine, and up to 3 session presets. No feature gating.

What is the Founding Member program?

For $99 one-time (launching Jan 9, 2026), you get lifetime access to all Pro and Teams features, plus exclusive perks like direct developer access.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, all subscriptions can be cancelled anytime. Your data remains accessible, and you can export your presets.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. No questions asked.
